## 🚀 About Me

I am a **Senior Power BI Developer & Data Analyst** with a proven track record of architecting end-to-end business intelligence solutions. Backed by a 55K+ LinkedIn network, I specialize in converting raw enterprise data into executive dashboards that drive strategic decision-making.

My expertise spans across semantic model optimization, writing complex DAX queries, and orchestrating data models in Power BI Service and Microsoft Fabric. I focus on delivering scalable, high-performance analytics to business leaders and operational teams.

---

## 📊 Power BI Expertise

Power BI is my core specialization. I build enterprise-grade analytics solutions focusing on:

- **Dashboard Development:** Creating interactive, executive-level KPI dashboards.
- **Data Modeling:** Architecting robust Star and Snowflake schemas.
- **DAX Optimization:** Writing and optimizing complex DAX calculations for large datasets.
- **Data Transformation:** Extracting and cleaning data using Power Query (M-Code).
- **Power BI Service:** Managing workspaces, configuring gateways, and implementing Row-Level Security (RLS).
